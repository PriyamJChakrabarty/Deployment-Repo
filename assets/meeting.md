“Good morning everyone. Today I’ll walk you through Zudia+, an AI-powered legal meeting analysis platform designed to turn live negotiation and compliance discussions into structured, actionable legal outputs.
The core problem we are solving is simple: in many organizations, important legal decisions are discussed verbally in meetings, but those decisions do not always get reflected quickly or accurately in contracts, clauses, and internal records. Zudia+ closes that gap.
Let me explain the workflow.
First, Zudia+ listens to a live meeting or takes a recorded meeting audio file. The system transcribes that conversation into text in near real time. This transcript becomes the foundation for all downstream analysis.
Second, once the transcript is available, the platform analyzes it for legal and compliance intelligence. It extracts key legal items such as the clause being discussed, a description of the issue, the level of risk, the responsible person, and the deadline. In parallel, it also generates clear action items so the team knows exactly what needs to happen next.
Third, Zudia+ works with legal source assets, especially PDF-based agreements and contract documents. When a PDF is uploaded, the system extracts the full text, identifies the document type, captures important metadata such as the parties involved, execution date, term length, termination notice period, fee structure, and confidentiality survival terms, and then breaks the document down into clause-level records.
Fourth, those documents are stored in a structured database. This means the platform is not just reading one file at a time. It is building a searchable legal memory. Users can open the database viewer, inspect documents, expand clauses, and review the exact text tied to each agreement.
Fifth, the most valuable part is the comparison layer. Zudia+ compares what people said in the meeting against what currently exists in the stored legal documents. If the meeting implies that a notice period should be shortened, a governing law clause should change, or a new clause like Force Majeure should be added, the platform proposes those updates automatically.
These suggestions are not applied blindly. The user can review them, select the valid ones, and then apply only the approved changes back into the document database. That gives teams both speed and control.
In addition to document intelligence, the project also supports a database assistant that can answer questions based on internal data sources. In the current implementation, that includes both legal document records and employee records. So a user can ask questions not just about clauses and contracts, but also about who in the organization is responsible, which team handles a matter, or what expertise exists internally.
There is also a legal assistant chatbot dedicated to legal, compliance, regulatory, contractual, and governance-related queries. This makes the product useful both during and after the meeting.
So to summarize, the main assets this platform is designed to analyze are live audio, recorded audio, meeting transcripts, legal issues, action items, PDFs, document metadata, legal clauses, full-text contract content, structured document databases, employee databases, and transcript-driven contract change suggestions.
The end result is a system that transforms unstructured legal conversation into structured compliance flags, accountable action items, searchable legal records, and proposed contractual updates.
If we were demonstrating the product live, the ideal demo flow would be:
start a meeting recording, allow transcript generation, trigger AI analysis, show compliance flags and action items, open the database viewer, upload or inspect a legal document, generate suggested changes from the transcript, review them with stakeholders, and apply the approved updates to the database.
That is the value of Zudia+: it connects conversation, compliance, and contracts in one workflow.”
